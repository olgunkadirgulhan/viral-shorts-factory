## Who I am talking to
25-40, follows stocks and crypto but can't read a chart, gives this 5 minutes a day. US/UK/global English speaker.

## Words I never use
"real-time" (data is delayed), "guaranteed", "to the moon", "can't lose", "game-changer", "let's dive in",
"buy now", "sell now", "price target", "financial freedom in X days"

## What I will not claim
Price predictions, buy/sell advice, any number that is not in today's data. Every video says "Not financial advice".
Outrage/surprise angles never slide into misleading claims: only the real move in the data is described.

## My format
25-40s vertical. Hook lands in the first 1.5s, moving chart at frame 0. Always a chart.
No channel intro. One CTA at the end. Understandable on mute (on-screen text every beat).
Plain spoken English, "you" address, short sentences, no jargon without a 3-word explanation.
